Padur – A Blissful Destination for Senior Living

Padur is a picturesque place located in OMR, Chennai. It is well known for its serenity and is a perfect destination for Nature lovers.

Padur enjoys excellent connectivity to Highways. It houses temples , Malls with theatres and top notch health care facilitators providing an ideal balance of facilities needed for senior living residents.

The neighborhood promises a sense of calmness with its access to Mutukadu boat house, making it the first choice for a blissful retirement living.
