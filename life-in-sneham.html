Life at Sneham

Sneham Senior Living has multilateral platforms where we host various for seniors based on their preferences and dreams.

SatSangh

This facilitates spiritual gatherings, storytelling, singing, and cultural discussions that enrich the soul.
Fitness

Join fitness challenges and regular sessions like yoga, morning walks, and breathing exercises to keep energized.
Art

Explore your creative side through painting, crafting, music, drama, and more. Let your imagination fly.
Charity

Give back to the community through social work, donations, food drives, and helping the needy with love.

SatSungh

This facilitates various activities including:

Sloka Classes

Learn spiritual and traditional Slokas that bring peace and focus.

Veda Classes

Dive into the knowledge of the Vedas guided by learned scholars.

Bhajan/Harikatha

Participate in devotional Bhajans and storytelling sessions.

Smart Phone Classes

Get trained on using mobile phones and technology smartly.

Other Activities

Enjoy other community-centered events and interactive sessions.

Fitness

Fitness comes first! We are thrilled to challenge ourselves and gain control over our body to achieve our goals. Our Fitness includes:

Meditation

Relax your mind and discover inner peace through guided meditation sessions

Yoga

Improve flexibility and posture while calming the mind through Yoga practices

Physiotherapy

Strengthen muscles and manage pain with professional physiotherapy guidance

Walking / Jogging

Stay active and energetic with regular walking and jogging routines in groups

Swimming

Enjoy refreshing and safe swimming sessions designed to promote relaxation, fitness, and overall well-being in a supervised environment

Art

Art has its own wings – a space to express and explore creativity through:

Drawing

Unleash your imagination with colors, sketches, and beautiful artworks

Cooking

Cook and share your delicious recipes while learning from others

Singing

Sing your heart out and enjoy group karaoke or solo performances

Instruments

Explore musical instruments and practice with fellow enthusiasts

Charity

Charity keeps us growing – together we make a meaningful impact through:

Vidya Daan

Teach students during your leisure time and support their academic growth

Career Guidance

Mentor youth with professional advice, guidance, and counseling support

Abhayadana

Join in protecting and rescuing cows and supporting animal welfare initiatives

Other Charities

Engage in any social or charitable activity that aligns with your passion
