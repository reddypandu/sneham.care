Frequently Asked Questions

WHAT MAKES SNEHAM DIFFERENT FROM OTHER ASSISTED LIVING FACILITIES? +
Sneham takes pride in providing personalized care that is tailored to the unique needs and preferences of each resident. We offer a warm and hospitable environment that promotes privacy, independence, dignity, and quality of life
WHAT HEALTHCARE SERVICES AND AMENITIES ARE AVAILABLE AT SNEHAM? +
• Satvik Food • Spacious rooms with elderly-friendly amenities • Nursing station • On-call Doctor • Short-term stay • Medication management or supervision • Pharmacy & Lab collection services • Financial planning services • Exclusive Personal Assistance Services • Fitness and balance programs • Enriching activities and social events • Housekeeping and laundry services on-site .
WHAT SAFETY MEASURES ARE IN PLACE AT SNEHAM? +
We have a 24/7 emergency call system, secured private entrances and exits, and routine safety inspections to ensure that our facility is safe and secure for our residents. We also have trained staff members who are available to assist during needy times
HOW DO YOU INVOLVE FAMILIES IN THE CARE OF RESIDENTS? +
We encourage family involvement in the care of residents and maintain open communication channels with family members. We organize family gatherings, meetings, and educational sessions to keep families informed and engaged in their loved one's care journey. Family whatsapp group is created for clear and open communication with our Multi-disciplinary team involving doctor,physiotherapists and nurses
DO YOU OFFER SHORT-TERM STAYS? +
Yes, we offer short-term stays for individuals who may need temporary assistance due to illness, recovery from surgery, or when primary caregivers are unavailable
CAN THE ROOM BE PERSONALIZED WITH OUR OWN FURNITURE? +
Yes, absolutely. Residents are welcome to bring personal furniture, keepsakes, and decorative items. Familiar surroundings can greatly enhance emotional comfort and help make the space truly feel like home. We request you to let us know in advance to plan your stay
WHAT KIND OF SUPPORT DO ELDERLY RESIDENTS TYPICALLY NEED? +
Support needs may include: • Daily care: bathing, mobility, grooming • Medical monitoring: Medication, vitals, chronic conditions • Nutrition: Balanced meals and dietary planning • Social & emotional well-being: Group activities, companionship • Physical well-being: Yoga, Swimming and water aerobics, in consultation with general practitioner • Self development: Learning a forgotten hobby, awareness of latest technology • Safety & supervision: Fall prevention, emergency care • Transport & logistics: Appointments and errands • Financial guidance: Assistance with bills and insurance (if applicable) • Compliance: Existence certificate, Bank Compliance etc.
WHAT IS THE COST OF STAY AT YOUR ASSISTED LIVING FACILITY? +
Our pricing is tailored to the level of care and services required by each resident. We offer transparent and optimal pricing and will work with you to find the best price. Also our facility offers flexi-rental solutions to choose from per day charges to monthly charges
